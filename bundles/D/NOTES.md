# D_lr2e-4 notes
Run: rank 16, LR 2e-4 (A_base uses 1e-4), train_v1 (147 images), 1600 steps, seed 42, bf16, T4 GPU. Final checkpoint-1600; checkpoints every 200 steps.

Speed / VRAM / disconnects: about 8-10 s per optimizer step when a single run was active (7.9-9.7 observed). Peak VRAM about 6 GB (sampled with nvidia-smi, not a true peak). Total GPU training time about 300 min (approximate), spread over 3 sessions. Colab disconnected several times and GPU quota ran out once; the run was resumed from checkpoints at steps 400, 1000 and 1200 (the last session on a second account), which is why launch_cmd.sh shows --resume_from_checkpoint=latest. Twice a second training process was started by mistake on the same GPU (about 18 s/it while it lasted); it was stopped and the saved checkpoints were not affected.

What I saw in the validation images: at step ~1400 a strong Ravi Varma look (warm ochre/maroon palette, draped saris, jewelry, oil texture), but saturated colours and distorted faces and hands. One later validation image was blacked out by the safety checker (a false positive on a painting). The sweep on checkpoint-1600 shows the painting style already present at low LoRA strength and distortions (stretched figures, odd faces) at the highest strengths, so the best-looking range is the middle strengths, not the maximum.

Eval numbers (deep stage, from eval/results.json): style_margin rises from 0.123 (step 800) to 0.127 (1400) to 0.133 (1600), differences small relative to the spread (std about 0.035). max_train_dino_p95 (copying): across all screened checkpoints it rises from 0.80 (step 200) to about 0.83 by step 600 and then plateaus; among the three deep-scored checkpoints it is flat (0.846 -> 0.841). Most of the style is learned by step 400-800 (screen style_margin 0.081 at 200, 0.113 at 400, ~0.13 at 800 and later); later steps add little, and clip_text is highest early (0.315 at 400) and drifts to ~0.29. Holdout loss is nearly flat (0.2008 -> 0.2006) with a steady gap to the training probe (0.188 -> 0.187): no overfitting signal in the loss numbers. drift 0.165 -> 0.156; style bleed small (0.011-0.014). clip_text drops a little (0.300 -> 0.292). style_margin_no_trigger grows (0.016 -> 0.041): the style starts to appear without the trigger. Caution: the holdout captions used for the eval contain the v2 style suffix while this run was trained without it (v1 captions), and style_margin_suffix_only is 0.114 vs 0.133 with the trigger, so the suffix alone brings out most of the style in this LoRA.
